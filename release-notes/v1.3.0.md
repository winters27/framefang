Point at a window and Framefang lights it up. Click, and that window is your screenshot.

## New

- **Window and screen capture.** The window under your mouse lights up, with a blue frame and a tab naming it and its size. The frame glides from window to window as you move. Click to take it, or drag to draw a region as before. Over the desktop, the whole screen lights up.
- **Capture modes.** A bar at the top of the capture screen picks what a click takes: **Region or window**, **Screen**, or **All screens** (with more than one monitor). Press Tab to switch. Framefang remembers your choice.
- **Ctrl+A** takes the whole screen you're on. Press it again for every screen.
- **Any color.** Under the color swatches there's now a spectrum for any color, and an eyedropper that picks from anywhere on your screen. While you pick, a zoomed lens shows the exact pixel, next to a card with the color's swatch, HEX, RGB and HSL. Your color is kept for the next capture.
- **Save as.** Ctrl+Shift+S, or Shift-click Save, lets you pick the name, the folder, and PNG or JPEG.
- **"Copied to clipboard"** now shows for a moment when a copy closes the capture, so you know it worked.
- **Include the mouse pointer.** A new Settings switch puts the pointer in your screenshots.

## Changed

- The tools sit at the bottom right of your capture, next to Pin, Copy and Save, so they stay close on small captures.

## Updating from 1.2.0

Use **Check for updates** in the tray, or download `Framefang.msi` below and run it.

## Files

- `Framefang.msi`: the installer.
- `framefang-windows-x86_64.zip`: portable, just `framefang.exe`.
- `SHA256SUMS`: checksums the in-app updater verifies before installing.
