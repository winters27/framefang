Scrolling capture, a highlighter that reads the words, text and QR codes from the screen, step styles, a redrawn icon set, and a Frame Fang written from the ground up as its own code.

## New

- **Scrolling capture (Windows).** Press Tab to SCROLL mode, point at a window and the part that scrolls lights up. Click it (or drag over any area), then scroll it yourself, down or up, at your own pace. A counter shows how tall the picture is; scroll too fast and it asks you to go back a little. Enter or a click finishes, Esc cancels. Sticky headers show once and the scrollbar is left out. A page taller than the screen opens in a scrolling editor with a page map beside it: the wheel scrolls, a click on the map jumps, and drawing near an edge scrolls the page along.
- **Highlight text (H).** The Highlight tool reads the words in the picture and snaps to them: press on a word, drag to another, and everything between lights up line by line, the way selecting text does. Six looks: marker, clean, swipe, underline, circle and glow, in any color. On dark pages it lights the page instead of darkening it, so the words stay readable.
- **Copy text from the screen.** TEXT mode reads the words in a region and copies them, offline, with the system's own text recognition (Windows and macOS built in; on Linux it uses `tesseract` when installed).
- **Read QR codes.** QR mode copies what a QR code holds: a link, a Wi-Fi password, an email address or plain text. Links can open in your browser instead (Settings), and holding Ctrl as you let go does the other one.
- **Numbered steps in nine styles**: disc, ring, outline, square, halo, pin, hollow, pill and a terminal tag, previewed in the Step tool's panel. Color the body, ring and digits separately, and turn the shadow on or off.
- **A redrawn icon set**, one refined line style across the editor, with a new Pen icon.
- **Recent captures.** Your last 10 captures, copy-only ones too, wait in the tray menu. Click one to copy it again, Shift+click to edit it. Turning it off in Settings deletes them.
- **Capture last region**, from the tray or its own hotkey.
- **Record to GIF**, straight from the recorder.
- **Crop (C), spotlight (F) and magnify (Z)** in the editor.
- **Border or shadow** on every saved or copied screenshot.
- **File names your way**: date, app and counter, or your own pattern in settings.json. Two captures in one second never overwrite each other.

## Changed

- **Frame Fang is all its own code now.** Every part of the app was rewritten from scratch: the editor, the recorder, the tray, Settings, the dialogs and the core. It behaves the same, is better organized (the editor, tray, Settings and drawing code are now split into focused modules) and has many more tests, and it builds with no compiler warnings.
- **Settings**: a General tab; **After a scrolling capture** with its own hold key; **After a capture** now covers auto-copy; pick a particular FFmpeg on the FFmpeg row; section rules instead of boxes, and pixel-font headings in the title bar.
- **Clearer wording** in places: the editor's Pin button says "Pin over the screen", the video timeline says "Scrub through the clip", the discard prompts say what you'd lose ("Throw away 3 annotations?", "Close without saving your marks?"), and error messages say what went wrong and what to try in plain words.
- **About and Welcome** are simpler, with "Source on GitHub" and "Look for updates" buttons.
- **Updates** says "fetching..." while it checks, and "See all releases" opens the releases page.

## Fixed

- Toolbar tips sit under their own button.
- The scroll indicator shows, half-painted frames are never stitched, and Enter and Esc never reach the page you're scrolling.
- A release date with an unusual character no longer crashes the Updates window.
- A failed frame grab no longer leaves a temporary picture behind.

## Updating

From 1.4.0 or later, use **Check for updates** in the tray and press **Update now**: Frame Fang downloads, installs and reopens by itself.

From 1.3.0 or earlier, your copy still has the old updater, so this update goes the old way once: use **Check for updates** and follow the installer, or download `Framefang.msi` below and run it.

## Files

- `Framefang.msi`: the Windows installer.
- `Framefang-portable.exe`: Windows, runs without installing.
- `Framefang-macos-arm64.dmg`: macOS on Apple silicon (M1 and later).
- `Framefang-macos-x64.dmg`: macOS on Intel.
- `Framefang-linux-x86_64.AppImage`: Linux, one file. Make it executable, then run it.
- `Framefang-linux-x86_64-portable.tar.gz` and `Framefang-linux-arm64-portable.tar.gz`: Linux, the bare app.
- `SHA256SUMS`: checksums the in-app updater verifies.

The Mac app isn't signed by Apple yet. The first time you open it, macOS blocks it: go to **System Settings > Privacy & Security** and click **Open Anyway**.
