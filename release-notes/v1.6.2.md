A new capture dock, keys drawn as keycaps across the app, and the capture now opens over the Start menu.

## New

- **A capture dock you can't miss.** When you press Print Screen, a one-line dock at the bottom of the screen shows how to switch between Region, Screen and All screens. The Tab key sits on its left, drawn as a key, and taps down each time you press it. The dock sweeps in when the capture opens, and the mode name decodes in place when you switch. Esc and Close sit on its right.
- **Keycaps everywhere.** Shortcut keys show as small keycaps instead of plain text: in the editor's tooltips, the notices in the editor, the color picker, the tray menu, Settings (including your hotkeys inside their boxes) and the welcome window.

## Changed

- **Tooltips you'll notice.** The editor's tooltips are drawn in the same dark style as the rest of the app, with a colored edge, and show each tool's key, like `Pen [P]` or `Undo [Ctrl]+[Z]`.
- **The dock stays clear of the taskbar.** It sits above the taskbar on whichever screen you're capturing.

## Fixed

- **The capture opens over the Start menu (Windows).** With Start, search, the notification center or quick settings open, the capture screen used to appear underneath them. The screen is still frozen with the menu in the picture, and Frame Fang now closes the menu so the capture screen is on top.

## Updating

From 1.4.0 or later, use **Check for updates** in the tray and press **Update now**: Frame Fang downloads, installs and reopens by itself.

From 1.3.0 or earlier, your copy still has the old updater, so this update goes the old way once: use **Check for updates** and follow the installer, or download `Framefang.msi` below and run it.

## Files

- `Framefang.msi`: the Windows installer.
- `Framefang-portable.exe`: Windows, runs without installing.
- `Framefang-macos-arm64.dmg`: macOS on Apple silicon (M1 and later).
- `Framefang-macos-x64.dmg`: macOS on Intel.
- `Framefang-linux-x86_64.AppImage`: Linux, one file. Make it executable, then run it.
- `Framefang-linux-x86_64-portable.tar.gz` and `Framefang-linux-arm64-portable.tar.gz`: Linux, the bare app.
- `SHA256SUMS`: checksums the in-app updater verifies.

The Mac app isn't signed by Apple yet. The first time you open it, macOS blocks it: go to **System Settings > Privacy & Security** and click **Open Anyway**.
