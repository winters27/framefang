Captures can go straight to your clipboard, and the capture screen now opens in one piece.

## New

- **Copy only.** A new Settings row, **After a capture**, picks what happens when you let go of the drag: open the editor, as before, or copy the region and close, ready to paste.
- **Switch for one capture.** Hold Shift, Ctrl or Alt (pick which in Settings) with your capture key, or while you let go of the drag, to get the other choice for that capture.

## Fixed

- The capture screen and the editor no longer pop up small and grow out from the top left corner. They appear all at once, already in place.

## Updating from 1.1.0

Use **Check for updates** in the tray, or download `Framefang.msi` below and run it.

## Files

- `Framefang.msi`: the installer.
- `framefang-windows-x86_64.zip`: portable, just `framefang.exe`.
- `SHA256SUMS`: checksums the in-app updater verifies before installing.
