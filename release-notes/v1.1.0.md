Any key can take screenshots, Settings explains Windows' Print Screen switch clearly, Framefang can fetch FFmpeg for you, and the app has a sharper terminal look.

## New

- **Any key as your hotkey.** Rebind now takes whatever you press, alone or with modifiers: F1 to F24, Pause, Scroll Lock, Insert, the Menu key, numpad keys, punctuation, and media, volume and browser keys. A typing key (a letter, digit, arrow, Space and so on) needs Ctrl, Alt or Win with it, so it never stops that key typing; the hotkey box tells you what to hold.
- **A clear guide for Windows' Print Screen switch.** While Print Screen is your capture key, Settings shows whether Windows is taking it, the exact page (Bluetooth & devices > Keyboard), and the switch to turn off, quoted in your own Windows' words, with a button that opens it. It updates when you come back, and disappears when you bind another key.
- **FFmpeg when you need it.** Recording and video conversion use the FFmpeg you already have. If there is none, Framefang offers a one-click download of its own trimmed build (about 10 MB) from Settings, or as soon as you start a recording or open Convert Video. It is checked against its published checksum before it is installed.
- **A sharper look.** Window headings glow in your color scheme, sections read like a terminal (01 // CAPTURES), and the backgrounds carry faint scanlines.

## Fixed

- Pause and Scroll Lock could be picked as hotkeys but never registered; they work now.
- Shift alone no longer counts as a modifier for a typing key (Shift+S would have blocked typing a capital S).
- The installer's license page names Framefang's copyright.
- The in-app updater unpacks downloads with Windows' own tar, which fixes a silent failure when Framefang was started from a PowerShell 7 window.

## Updating from 1.0.0

Use **Check for updates** in the tray, or download `Framefang.msi` below and run it.

## Files

- `Framefang.msi`: the installer.
- `framefang-windows-x86_64.zip`: portable, just `framefang.exe`.
- `SHA256SUMS`: checksums the in-app updater verifies before installing.
